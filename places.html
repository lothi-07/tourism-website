Famous Places in Ooty

Ooty Lake

Ooty Lake is one of the most popular tourist attractions, ideal for relaxing boat rides.

Doddabetta Peak

Doddabetta Peak offers breathtaking panoramic mountain views and cool, fresh air.

Rose Garden

The Government Rose Garden features thousands of rose varieties in vibrant colors.

Pykara Waterfalls

Pykara Waterfalls is a scenic natural spot known for its peaceful surroundings and beauty.

Nilgiri Mountain Railways

The Nilgiri Mountain Railway is a historic UNESCO-listed hill railway in Tamil Nadu.

Botanical Garden

The Botanical Garden is a house pf huge variety of plants, indigenous species and orchids.
