About Ooty

Ooty is a beautiful hill station in Tamil Nadu, known for its cool climate, tea gardens, misty mountains, and scenic beauty throughout the year.

Why Tourists Love Ooty

Cool Weather

Enjoy pleasant temperatures and fresh mountain air in every season.

Natural Beauty

From lakes to peaks, every corner of Ooty offers stunning views.

Memorable Experiences

Take scenic train rides, explore gardens, and taste local tea.
